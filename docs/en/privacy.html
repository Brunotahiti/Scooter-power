Scooter Power Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Scooter Power is an iPhone app that displays a scooter trip dashboard. It is published by Bruno Seite.

In short

Everything Scooter Power measures stays on your iPhone, except during a group ride, if you join one: your location is then shared with the other riders of the group through iCloud, and deleted when you leave the ride; and, only if you turn it on, your best acceleration times are published in the leaderboards. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics.

Location is used while a trip is being recorded, including when the screen is locked or the app is in the background, so the trip is not interrupted. It is no longer used once you end the trip.

Trip history

Each finished trip is saved on your iPhone: date, statistics and route. You can delete a trip by swiping it in the history. Deleting the app erases the whole history.

If iCloud or computer backup is turned on for your iPhone, the app's data may be included in that backup, which Apple manages under its own rules.

Rider challenge

You can share a performance card with another rider, by QR code or message. It contains the nickname you chose, the name of your scooter setup and your performance statistics (acceleration times, speeds, thrust, lean angle). It contains neither your location, nor your routes, nor any identifier.

Sharing only happens when you decide, directly between you and the person of your choice: the card does not go through any server of the publisher. Cards you receive are kept on your iPhone and can be deleted at any time from the Statistics page.

Group ride

If you start or join a group ride (up to 5 riders, with a QR code), the app sends every few seconds during the trip your location, speed, heading, lean angle, acceleration and the nickname you chose. This data is stored in the app's public iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit service), under a random ride identifier and a random rider identifier, not linked to your Apple Account. Only people who have the ride's QR code can read it.

So that everyone can replay the ride, each participant keeps the trips of all the riders of the ride (nickname, route, speed, lean angle, acceleration) in the history on their own iPhone; they can delete it by swiping it.

Your location is deleted when you leave the ride. Nothing is sent outside a group ride. The publisher uses this data for no purpose other than displaying the ride. You must be signed in to iCloud to join a ride.

Leaderboards

Leaderboards are off by default. If you turn on “Publish my times”, the app publishes, for each acceleration range, your best time, its date, the nickname you chose and the make and model of your scooter, entered in the Garage. This data is stored in the app's public iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit service) under a random rider identifier, not linked to your Apple Account, and can be read by all users of the app. It contains neither your location nor your routes.

Turning the option off removes your times from the leaderboards.

Live location for your loved ones

This option is off by default. If you turn on “Share my trips live”, during each trip the app sends every 10 seconds your position, speed, heading, the recent route of the trip, the nickname you chose and the trip status (riding, paused, arrived, crash alert) to the app's public iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit service), under a random identifier not linked to your Apple Account. Anyone who has your tracking link can view this data on a web page while it is available. The page is not listed by search engines.

Turning the option off or creating a new link deletes the shared data. Outside a trip, nothing is sent.

Crash detection and emergency contacts

During a trip, the app watches motion sensors and speed to detect a possible fall. This analysis happens only on your iPhone. If a fall is detected, the app sounds an alarm and offers to send a text message, which you send yourself, to the emergency contacts you chose, with a map link to your position. These contacts (name and phone number) are kept on your iPhone only; the app does not read your address book beyond the contact you pick. Nothing is sent automatically.

Garage, maintenance and fuel

Your scooters, their mileage, maintenance items and fill-ups are kept on your iPhone only. Maintenance reminders are local notifications created by the iPhone. Voice announcements are spoken by the iPhone, without any server.

Siri, Shortcuts and widget

Siri and Shortcuts actions (start, pause, end a trip) and the home screen widget run on your iPhone. The widget reads a summary of your recent trips that the app shares with it on the iPhone only.

Sending a trip to a friend

You can send a trip to a friend so they can replay it on the map. The file contains the nickname you chose, the name of your scooter setup, the date, the trip's GPS route and, along that route, speed, acceleration and lean angle. It contains no identifier.

The route reveals places you went through, such as your starting point: only send a trip to people you trust. Sending only happens when you decide, by the means of your choice (AirDrop, Messages…), without going through any server of the publisher. Trips you receive stay on your iPhone and can be deleted by swiping them in the history.

Sharing with third parties

The publisher does not sell or share any data. Outside the group rides, live location and leaderboards described above, no data leaves your iPhone without your action.

The map uses Apple Maps: to load map tiles, your iPhone tells Apple which area is displayed. This is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

Your choices

You can withdraw access to location, motion, camera or photos at any time in Settings → Scooter Power. Without location access, the app cannot record a trip.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data about them.
