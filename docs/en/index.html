Scooter Power Support

Scooter Power is an iPhone app that turns your phone into a scooter dashboard: GPS speed, acceleration thrust, lean angle, standing-start and roll-on times, records, statistics and trip history.

Contact us

A question, a problem or a suggestion? Write to contact@manaprocess.com. Please mention your iPhone model and iOS version: it helps us answer faster.

Getting started

  • Mount the phone firmly on the handlebar, screen facing you.
  • With the scooter upright and stopped, tap “Start trip”. The phone's position at that moment is the reference for the lean angle.
  • Allow access to location and motion the first time.
  • When you arrive, tap “End trip”. The trip is saved in the history, available from the clock icon.

Frequently asked questions

The speed shows “--”

The app has not received an accurate GPS position for a few seconds. This is normal at startup, indoors, in a tunnel or between tall buildings. Also check that location is allowed, with “Precise Location” on, in Settings → Scooter Power → Location.

The trip does not start

Location access was probably denied. Open Settings → Scooter Power → Location and choose “While Using the App”.

The lean angle looks off

The phone has moved in its mount. With the scooter upright, open the “•••” menu at the top of the screen and tap “Recalibrate lean angle”.

Acceleration times stay empty

Standing starts (0–30, 0–50, 0–80, 0–100 km/h) are only measured after a complete stop. Roll-ons (50–90, 50–100 km/h) start the moment you pass 50 km/h while accelerating. In both cases the acceleration must be continuous: the timer is cancelled if you slow down. Since GPS only provides about one position per second, times are indicative.

Nothing shows on the Lock Screen

Check that Live Activities are allowed in Settings → Scooter Power.

How do I change the language?

Scooter Power is available in English and French. It follows your iPhone's language. To choose a different language for the app only, open Settings → Scooter Power → Language, or tap “Language” in the app's settings.

The battery drains quickly

The app uses GPS continuously and keeps the screen on during a trip. On a long ride, plug in your phone.

Deleting my data

Swipe a trip to the left in the history to delete it. Deleting the app erases all trips. No data is kept anywhere other than on your iPhone.

Safety

Never operate the app while riding. Readings are indicative and do not replace the vehicle's speedometer. Acceleration timing is only for places where it is legal and safe.
