## Description

Scooter Power turns your iPhone into a neon performance dashboard for scooters and maxi-scooters. Mount your phone on the handlebar, tap "Start trip" and watch everything live.

RIDE TOGETHER
• Group rides: one rider shows a QR code, up to 4 friends scan it, and everyone appears on the same live map
• Each rider has their own color, glowing trail, name tag and live speed
• Group rides are saved so you can replay them with every rider in sync
• Send any trip to a friend and race against their ghost on the map

LIVE DASHBOARD
• Neon GPS speedometer whose color shifts from cyan to red as your speed rises
• Rear-view scooter on a road that scrolls at your speed: it leans into corners, squats when you accelerate and lights its brake light when you brake
• Acceleration and braking thrust in m/s² with a 30-second graph
• Lean angle left and right, computed from the gyroscope and your speed
• Top and average speed, distance, altitude, riding time, GPS signal

0–60 AND ROLL-ONS
• Standing starts timed automatically: 0–20, 0–30, 0–50 and 0–60 mph (or 0–30, 0–50, 0–80, 0–100 km/h)
• Roll-ons: 30–50 and 30–60 mph (or 50–90 and 50–100 km/h)
• "New record" appears the moment you beat your best
• Trophies in the trip summary, and a map showing where your best accelerations happened

REPLAY EVERY RIDE
• Relive any trip from above: the scooter follows your route at real speed, turns, leans and brakes
• Live speed, thrust and lean during playback, from 1x to 20x

STATS AND MODS
• Full performance stats: records and averages for every interval, top speed, totals
• Modified your scooter? Name each setup ("Stock", "Sport variator"…) and compare any two, figure by figure, in percent (+10%, −5%…)
• Compare two trips side by side
• Share a performance card by QR code and compare yourself with another rider

ALWAYS VISIBLE
• Live Activity on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island
• Landscape dashboard
• Keeps recording with the screen locked

SETTINGS
• Miles or kilometres, English or French
• Speed alert: the speedometer turns red and the phone vibrates above your limit

YOUR DATA
No account, no ads, no analytics. Your trips stay on your iPhone. During a group ride only, your position is shared with the riders of that ride through iCloud and deleted when you leave.

GOOD TO KNOW
• Readings come from the phone's GPS and sensors. They are indicative and do not replace your vehicle's speedometer.
• The phone must be firmly mounted on the handlebar. Never use the app while riding.
• Only time accelerations where it is legal and safe.
• Group rides require iCloud and a data connection.
• Continuous GPS use in the background can reduce battery life.
